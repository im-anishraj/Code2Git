# Privacy Policy for Code2Git

**Effective Date:** September 22, 2026

Code2Git ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
Code2Git operates entirely within your browser and does not send your personal data to any external servers owned by us. To function, it requires access to:
*   **GitHub Authentication:** We use OAuth to authenticate with your GitHub account. Your OAuth tokens are stored securely and locally in your browser to push code on your behalf.
*   **LeetCode / GeeksforGeeks Data:** The extension reads your submitted code directly from your browser when you solve a problem to automate the commit process.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
*   **GitHub Access:** We use your GitHub access token exclusively to create repositories, make commits, and manage the code you solve on supported platforms.
*   **Local Processing:** All data processing (parsing problem names, difficulty, and your code) happens locally within your browser.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We **do not** sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data flows directly from your browser to GitHub's servers via their official API. We do not host any backend databases that store your code or user information.

## 4. Security
We prioritize your privacy by storing your GitHub OAuth tokens using the secure, local storage mechanisms provided by your browser. We strongly recommend that you do not share your tokens or authorize untrusted applications.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and publishing the changes in our repository.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/im-anishraj/Code2Git).
